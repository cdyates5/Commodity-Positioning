Method

Positioning: for each weekly CFTC report, net length = managed money longs − shorts; the group reading sums net length across constituents and divides by summed managed money longs + shorts — the net directional skew of speculative positioning, bounded ±100%.

Price index: constituents weighted by full-sample average dollar open interest (avg contracts × avg price × contract size), each rebased to 100 at 2006-06-13. Crude carries the energy index; gold carries metals — as in production-weighted benchmarks.
